# Genre-Specific Mixing & Production Targets

## HINDI / BOLLYWOOD

### Loudness & Dynamics
- Integrated LUFS: -9 to -11 LUFS (streaming: Spotify normalises to -14, JioSaavn -12)
- True Peak ceiling: -1.0 dBTP
- Dynamic range: 6–8 LU (some compression but not over-squashed)

### Frequency Profile
- Sub bass (20–60Hz): Present but controlled — not as dominant as trap
- Bass (60–200Hz): Warm and full, sits under kick
- Low-mids (200–500Hz): Clean — watch for tabla/dholak mud here
- Mids (500Hz–2kHz): Vocals forward, melodic instruments present
- Presence (2–5kHz): Vocal clarity zone — protect this frequency range
- Air (8–16kHz): Smooth shimmer, not harsh

### Vocal Treatment (Bollywood standard)
- Compression: 4–6dB GR, slow attack, auto/medium release (LA-2A character)
- De-essing: gentle, -3 to -5dB reduction only
- Reverb: Plate or Hall, pre-delay 20–30ms, decay 1.2–2.0s
- Doubling: subtle chorus or Little Microshift width (not heavy doubling)
- Tuning: Melodyne — natural, breath-preserving, not robotic

### Arrangement Notes
- Intros often feature solo instruments (sitar, piano, strings) before full arrangement
- Chorus density increases significantly — build energy through layering
- String swell on pre-chorus is a hallmark — use Cinematic Studio Strings or Symphobia 2
- Tabla/percussion often panned slightly off-centre for width

### Reference Artists
- Arijit Singh mixes: warm, intimate, vocal-forward
- A.R. Rahman mixes: experimental spatial processing, heavy reverb on atmospheres
- Pritam: punchy, modern Bollywood with Western pop influence

## CINEMATIC / FILM SCORE

### Loudness & Dynamics
- Integrated LUFS: -18 to -23 LUFS (dialogue headroom, dynamic storytelling)
- True Peak: -3.0 dBTP (more headroom for peaks)
- Dynamic range: 10–20+ LU (contrast is the point — quiet → loud moments)

### Frequency Profile
- Sub (20–40Hz): Intentional for tension/impact moments — use sparingly
- Bass (40–120Hz): Cellos, basses, brass low-end — rich and powerful
- Mids: Often scooped in large orchestral moments for clarity
- Presence: Restrained — let the music breathe
- Air: Spacious, room reverb baked in

### Orchestral Mixing Priorities
- **Depth:** Use reverb to place instruments at different distances
  - Close: Strings, solo instruments (shorter reverb, 0.8–1.2s)
  - Mid: Brass, woodwinds (medium reverb, 1.5–2.0s)
  - Far: Choir, full orchestra (long reverb, 2.5–4.0s)
- **Width:** Strings typically left-heavy, brass right-heavy (traditional orchestra spread)
- **Layering:** Nucleus or Metropolis Ark 4 for large ensemble impact hits

### Adi's Best Libraries for This Genre
- Emotional Cello + Emotional Viola: intimate, close-mic sorrow/tension
- Cinematic Studio Strings: modern cinematic standard
- Ólafur Arnalds Chamber Evolutions: evolving textures, Hans Zimmer-adjacent
- Symphobia 2: large orchestral swells, cinematic weight
- CineBrass + CineWinds: brass stingers, woodwind detail
- Epic Percussion 2 & 3 / Damage 2: impact hits, dramatic percussion
- Rise & Hit: transitions and tension builds

### Reference Composers
- Hans Zimmer: layered, synth-hybrid orchestral — The Legend HZ is built for this
- Ludwig Göransson: raw, intimate, unconventional orchestration
- Ennio Morricone: space and silence as compositional tools

## EDM / ELECTRONIC

### Loudness & Dynamics
- Integrated LUFS: -7 to -9 LUFS
- True Peak: -0.3 to -1.0 dBTP
- Dynamic range: 4–6 LU (heavily compressed, pumping sidechain is intentional)

### Frequency Profile
- Sub (20–60Hz): The foundation — kick sub and bass sub must NOT clash
  - Use Pro-Q 4 sidechain EQ: duck bass sub when kick hits
- Bass (60–150Hz): Tight, punchy, mono below 80Hz always
- Low-mids (150–500Hz): Clean, notch out mud aggressively
- Mids: Synth leads and stabs live here — Serum 2 / Omnisphere 3
- Highs: Bright, sparkly — helps energy on small speakers

### Key Techniques
- **Sidechain compression:** Kickstart 2 on bass/pads — the pump IS the groove
- **Mono bass:** Everything below 80Hz in mono (check with Pro-Q 4 stereo scope)
- **Shaperbox 3:** Rhythmic tremolo/volume shaping on synth stabs
- **Output Portal:** Granular texture on atmospheric layers
- **Serum 2:** Primary lead/bass design tool

### Drop vs. Build Arrangement
- Build: Remove low end, add tension (risers from Rise & Hit), filter sweeps
- Drop: Everything hits together — sub, kick, lead — maximum density
- Use Kickstart 2 for instant sidechain pumping on drops

## HIP-HOP / TRAP

### Loudness & Dynamics
- Integrated LUFS: -8 to -10 LUFS
- True Peak: -0.3 to -1.0 dBTP
- Dynamic range: 5–7 LU

### Frequency Profile
- 808 bass: Dominant — must tune 808 to key of song (use Melodyne 5)
- Kick: Short, punchy, 60–80Hz thud + 3–5kHz click
- Hi-hats: Crisp, present — often the rhythmic glue in trap
- Vocal: 3–5kHz presence boost, de-essed heavily, often heavily tuned
- Snare: 200Hz body + 5kHz crack

### 808 Mixing (Critical)
- Tune the 808 to song key using Melodyne 5 before any processing
- Keep 808 mostly mono, add subtle width above 200Hz only
- UAD 1176 on 808: fast attack (1–3), medium release (4–5), ratio 4:1
- Low-shelf boost at 60Hz for weight, high-pass at 30Hz to remove rumble
- 808 and kick should share the low end — sidechain kick → 808

### Vocal Treatment (Trap/Hip-Hop)
- Heavy tuning is stylistic — Melodyne or pitch correction more aggressive
- 1176 fast attack for transient snap on rap vocals
- UAD LA-2A for sustained sung phrases
- Waves CLA-2A or CLA-76 also appropriate from Waves Ultimate
- Plate reverb (short, 0.6–0.9s) + EchoBoy Jr for rhythmic delay throws

## AMBIENT / EXPERIMENTAL

### Loudness & Dynamics
- Integrated LUFS: -16 to -20 LUFS (dynamics are the art form)
- True Peak: -3.0 dBTP
- Dynamic range: 12–18+ LU

### Texture & Space Philosophy
- Reverb is not an effect — it IS the sound
- Long decay times (3–8 seconds) are normal
- Valhalla Shimmer for infinite, evolving tails
- Output Portal for granular transformation of any source

### Adi's Best Libraries for This Genre
- Ólafur Arnalds Chamber Evolutions: already ambient by design
- Soft String Textures: slow-attack bowed textures
- Folds, Frames, Strands, Void & Vista: purpose-built for ambient sound design
- Hearth and Hollow: intimate, fragile, experimental
- Heavyocity Convergence: hybrid cinematic/ambient textures

### Processing for Ambient
- Valhalla VintageVerb: Bright Hall or Noisefloor algorithm
- Valhalla Shimmer: pitch shift + infinite reverb for pads
- Gullfoss: intelligent tonal balancing on dense texture layers
- Shaperbox 3: subtle tremolo on pad layers for movement
- Pro-Q 4: gentle broad cuts, never surgical — preserve the wash

## CROSS-GENRE: HINDI + CINEMATIC (Fir Kyu territory)

This hybrid is Adi's primary creative zone. Key principles:

- **Emotional strings** (Emotional Cello/Viola) sit in the intimate close range
- **Full orchestral** (CSS, Symphobia) provides the swell behind
- **Vocal treatment** follows Bollywood standard (warm, present, tuned naturally)
- **Dynamics** follow cinematic (10–14 LU) — NOT compressed like commercial Bollywood
- **Reverb:** Two reverb buses — one for vocals (Plate, short), one for strings (Hall, long)
- **LUFS target:** -12 to -14 LUFS for this hybrid (streaming-ready, cinematic breathing room)
